Nyctron

An independent engineering and research project exploring quantitative finance, algorithmic trading, computational infrastructure, and AI-assisted research.

Nyctron is an independent engineering and research project. Its work sits at the intersection of quantitative finance, algorithmic trading, computational infrastructure, and AI-assisted research, with an emphasis on practical tools and reproducible methods.

The project is developing incrementally. Work is organised around questions that can be investigated with clear methods, documented assumptions, and results that others can inspect. Nothing here should be read as a claim that a product has launched, that research results have been achieved, or that capabilities exist beyond what is described.

Origin of the Name

The name Nyctron is invented. It was inspired by Nyctosaurus, an extinct genus of pterosaur — a flying reptile that lived alongside the dinosaurs but is not classified as one.

The reference is a matter of identity and tone rather than a scientific claim. It is not intended to imply any technical, historical, or biological connection to the animal.

Areas of Work

  1. 01

    Quantitative Research

    Methods, strategy evaluation, time-series analysis, and risk assessment, with assumptions stated explicitly.

  2. 02

    Computational Infrastructure

    Software and Linux-based environments that make quantitative workflows repeatable and easier to operate.

  3. 03

    AI-Assisted Tools

    Practical uses of artificial intelligence in research and engineering workflows, evaluated rather than assumed.

How We Work

  1. 01

    Reproducibility

    Methods, assumptions, and results should be inspectable and reproducible by someone else.

  2. 02

    Practical Engineering

    Tools are built to solve concrete problems, not to demonstrate technology for its own sake.

  3. 03

    Critical Evaluation

    Claims are tested and questioned, including our own, before they are repeated.

  4. 04

    Honest Documentation

    Limitations and open questions are documented alongside results and assumptions.
