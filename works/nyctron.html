Nyctron

An independent exploration of quantitative finance and computational systems.

Nyctron is an early-stage independent project exploring the intersection of quantitative finance, algorithmic trading, artificial intelligence, and computational infrastructure.

Its broader direction is to investigate how software and computational methods can support research and experimentation in financial markets.
